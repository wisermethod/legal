# Own pack questions

**Rules every question follows:**

- The session states no rule from its own memory. Every rule in the pack comes from what the person supplies.
- A rule the person gives without an official source is recorded `[verify]`, never `Verified`.
- The session never says what a rule means for the person's case. It records the rule and where it came from.

## Before the questions

> The plugin has no rules set for <court, forum or state>. You can keep your own in this case's folder. I'll record each rule exactly as its official source states it, with where it came from and the date you read it. Every answer that uses it will say it came from your own pack.

## The questions, asked once per rule

**1. What the rule says**

> What does the rule say? Copy the exact words from the source, and include the rule or section number.

**2. Where it comes from**

> Where did you read it? Give the link, or the title and section of the official publication, such as the state's statutes, the court's own rules page, or the arbitration organization's published rules. If it came from something else, such as an article, a guide or a lawyer's summary, tell me that instead.

*An official source is recorded as the source. Anything else is recorded as what it is, and the rule is marked `[verify]`.*

**3. When you read it**

> What date did you read it?

**4. A saved copy**

> I'll save the words you gave me in this case's folder, with the link and the date at the top, so the rule can be checked later. Is that the passage to keep?
>
> Yes · No, let me give the full passage

## After the last rule

> Your pack for <court, forum or state> has <n> rules: <n> from official sources, and <n> marked to confirm because they came from somewhere else. I'll flag any rule you read more than 180 days ago so you can check it again.
