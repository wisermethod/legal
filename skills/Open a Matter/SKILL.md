# Open a Matter

## Context

Use for "new case," "set up a case for," "file a new case," "start a lawsuit," "open a case" or "draft a complaint" in the litigant's own matter. Apply the legal constitution's Standing constraint. The matter record can hold any confirmed jurisdiction. The initiation procedure below covers Colorado district court civil cases of the class described in the court's current profile. Use Take Over a Matter for a populated legacy case folder.

Classifier seam: none.

## Objective

Create the matter's project and initialize its record. For an in-scope filing request, produce a reviewed initiation packet and, when the litigant reports filing and service, record the assigned number, proof and resulting deadlines.

## Inputs

- `<user_request>` identifies the scope, matter name, intended outcome and owning root or request for a dedicated user root.
- `<context>` provides known court or forum, jurisdiction, venue, procedural posture and filing build location. A records-only request can start before forum selection.
- `<source_material>` provides parties and contacts, claims and facts, evidence and dates, any prior case action, service information and current rules or official forms.

## Steps

Give the [Handoff label](../../AGENTS.md#handoff-label) in the handoff message whenever this work returns an assessment, packet or draft to the person, including on an early return. Never write it inside a document.

### What can proceed on this entry?

Follow [Litigation Counsel's preflight contract](../../experts/Litigation%20Counsel/EXPERT.md#how-does-a-skill-request-and-apply-preflight) before dependent work.

### What does this matter need to open?

1. **Place the project.** Which user root owns this matter under `wiser/standards/user-root.md` C3 and C4? In an existing owning root use `work/<slug>/`. If the person wants a dedicated user root for one matter, hand the root request to `wiser`'s Onboard Root by name, then use that root's `work/<slug>/`. If ownership or the destination is unsettled, ask before writing. Is the project path unoccupied, an established project for this case, or occupied by other material? For an unoccupied path, copy the bundled `assets/matter-template/` and complete the project `AGENTS.md` with the template’s single startup order, the glossary designation or statement of absence, forum and posture, authoritative paths, retained file map and the owner’s completion condition or pending choice. Read back the file set and keep the root declaration in the root. For a confirmed project for this case, read its declaration and use the current files. For a legacy case folder, use Take Over a Matter. For any other collision, stop and confirm a different destination before copying.
2. **Initialize the supplied facts.** For an existing matter, start with Maintain Matter File in preflight mode. Preserve confirmed entries and route updates to existing dates and events through its applicable operation. In a new record, populate the parties and service contacts, claims as asserted, facts, supporting records, amount, initial history, deadline rows in the template’s register shape and arguments narrative and positions. Identify each source and distinguish confirmed facts from reported or unknown ones. Enter an unknown forum, claim element or limitations basis as pending. Ask about any fact needed to resolve a potentially urgent date and surface it before returning the record. In history, or the matter's designated status record, record prepared, filed, accepted and served separately. Identify what has been prepared, use a dated filing confirmation for filed, use the acceptance record and assigned number for accepted, and use proof for served. Name which events and dates are still unconfirmed or untriggered. Update these states only when their evidence arrives.
3. **Decide whether the filing route applies.** What did the litigant request?
   - **Record setup alone.** Return the initialized record with unresolved forum, authority and deadlines visible. Leave forum selection and claim analysis for a request that needs them.
   - **Unclear scope.** Ask whether the litigant wants record setup, a complaint draft or a filing packet. Hold drafting and production pending the answer.
   - **Complaint draft or initiation packet.** What forum does the record establish? If county court, small claims, federal court or another state applies, state the scope gap and stop the Colorado district initiation procedure. For a complaint draft only, pass the initialized record and verified forum to Pleadings, subject to applicable preflight holds. For a full initiation request, return the initialized record with known dates and any verified forum requirements. If the forum or amount is unclear, use Venue Selection and ask for any missing facts. Hold work that depends on a court or amount until it is verified. If the governing forum is non-court, inventory its initiation instructions and identify each rule or form still needed. For the Colorado district civil route, confirm the working threshold of over $25,000 and the applicable forum through Venue Selection and the current rule before relying on them.

   For a requested draft or packet, use the applicable practice-area assessment and Claim Chart where element support is needed. Verify the elements, applicable jurisdiction and venue, amount in controversy, and each claim's limitation period, trigger and relevant dates before dependent drafting or filing. An unresolved trigger or rule, or a possible time bar, warrants a dismissal or sanction risk flag, limited-scope counsel or document review and a hold on the affected pleading. Apply any other preflight hold, including for financial exposure, complex procedure or matters beyond the litigant's comfort. Where preparation can proceed independently, record the supported portions and what would clear each hold. A hold on the pleading skips steps 4 through 6 for that pleading and keeps it out of any drafting or production handoff. Return the initialized record, risk flag and evidence needed to clear the hold. If the pleading can proceed and the confirmed forum is non-court, route the supported submission to Pleadings or Draft Motion according to the requested document. Use Produce Court Filing when a non-court artifact is requested and the applicable gates clear.

   For a confirmed or proposed Colorado district civil draft or packet, read the Legal Court Filing tool contract and run `node scripts/court-filing.js profiles` from its directory before drafting. In the returned `profiles` array, select the entry with `jurisdiction_id: "colorado-district"` and read `filing_packets.new_case_complaint`. When the forum is still proposed, use this as a requirements inventory and return the held checklist for a requested packet. Keep the supported preparation separate from dependent drafting, form completion and production.
4. **Build the complaint.** Read Pleadings as a requirements reference, including the C.R.C.P. 8 check where it governs. Open a Matter owns the complaint build for this direct initiation route. When Pleadings requested intake only, hand back the initialized record and rule findings so that caller can write the complaint. If the litigant brings a Pleadings draft for initiation, review and reuse that exact version where it meets the verified requirements. For a direct initiation that needs a new draft, write it here using Draft Motion’s Document registers and Filing prose checklist. Use Draft Motion for any separately requested motion. Use `body.md` for numbered supported factual allegations, separate counts with required elements, a jury demand when applicable and the prayer for relief. Read it aloud in a plain, informed self-represented voice. Preserve necessary pleading structure and correct citation form. Verify the court's AI disclosure requirements where applicable. Build `caption.json` from sourced intake and the Court Filing profile. Declare `document_type: "new-case-complaint"` and `filing_packet: "new_case_complaint"`. Leave `case_number` empty for assignment by the court. Use the assigned division or leave `division` empty when the confirmed court instructions accept an unassigned division. Apply the [initial complaint certificate check](#what-can-the-initial-complaint-certificate-state) to source the required `service` fields and determine what can be released. Run Verify Citations with the exact body and its cited-attachment manifest and resolve all `[verify]` flags. For a draft-only request, return the draft and holds without starting production. For a review artifact or initiation packet, check the certificate and citation gates before production. The profile’s null `length_presets` for `new-case-complaint` supplies the mechanical choice. If a rule or order sets a maximum, record it and hold production until the type and limit reconcile. If the checks clear, hand the approved draft, caption, clearance, manifest and exact attachments to Produce Court Filing for the complaint only. Its court PDF validation and human review are mandatory before filing. Stop at the review artifact if that is all the person requested.
5. **Prepare official forms and fees.** For an initiation packet, confirm current applicability and requirements with the Colorado Judicial Branch's authoritative materials, including current copies supplied by the litigant. Use its official fillable PDFs for JDF 601 District Court Civil Case Cover Sheet, associated with the initial pleading under C.R.C.P. 16.1 when applicable, and JDF 600 District Court Civil Summons. Fill every required field on the provided PDFs after the forum and form requirements are confirmed. Confirm the fee amount and, for a waiver, the current motion and financial affidavit forms `[verify]`. Which route did the litigant choose? If paying, include the fee as verified. If seeking waiver, include the verified motion and affidavit. If undecided, ask which route they want and hold completion of the packet. If any required fee, form or information remains unresolved or inaccessible, return the held packet checklist naming its source, what's missing and what to provide or verify. Use the court's original forms for the cover sheet and summons.
6. **Filing and service.** Before filing, verify the working 63-day interval for service after filing, the permitted method and proof under C.R.C.P. 4 and any order, and the applicable C.R.C.P. 12 answer period and computation basis below. If any is unresolved, hold filing. Show the validated complaint, official forms, payment or waiver materials, exact destination and attachments together. Apply Produce Court Filing’s exact-artifact preview and release steps to the entire packet, including the official forms’ sourced requirements gate. Record explicit approval against the hashes of the files shown. Recheck the hashes before any transmission. The litigant uses Colorado Courts E-Filing (CCEF) for an approved packet where applicable. On filing confirmation or receipt, route the confirmed filing to Maintain Matter File. On acceptance, update the parties record, caption and other affected case records with the assigned number. Have the litigant arrange service and file the return or proof after service. Apply Produce Court Filing's applicable gates before any filing or service transmission. Use [Returning the work](../AGENTS.md#returning-the-work) for the filing-draft handoff. Ask the litigant for the filing confirmation and, separately, for the proof after service.
7. **Seed deadlines.** After actual service, pass the proof to Maintain Matter File’s **Service confirmed** operation for location, date and method. Check the facts against the verified C.R.C.P. 12 calculation basis. The working Colorado district civil defaults are 21 days after in-state service and 35 days after out-of-state service. Use the applicable trigger, exceptions, time computation and any order. If service facts or calculation inputs remain uncertain, keep that deadline pending. Record case management, discovery and other deadlines as they arise. Route any confirmed proof filing to Maintain Matter File for history, index and deadline updates. If filing or service is still pending, list which dates are untriggered or awaiting confirmation.

### What can the initial complaint certificate state?

The Colorado district profile prints a certificate. Check the operative court instructions for what document delivery, if any, it certifies at initial filing. State only facts the signer can attest to about the identified document, recipient, permitted method and actual date of any delivery made. A blank date on a review draft is for later completion against actual evidence. If initial Rule 4 service is still ahead, leave it in the service plan and record its later proof separately. If the governing instructions do not resolve what can appear in the profiled certificate before process is served, return the draft or checklist held and name the court form, rule or authoritative direction needed for a truthful certificate or an accepted alternate procedure.

## Pitfalls

- An ambiguous name, scope, owning root or jurisdiction needs clarification before the dependent step. A request to draft or open a record does not authorize filing, paying or serving.
- Official forms may change. If the required official form cannot be obtained or checked, report the missing form and hold the packet. Never recreate a JDF form in Word or the filing renderer.
- A dormant or uncertain limitations date is consequential. Keep the basis and verification state in the deadline record and surface imminent or missed fatal dates first.

## Success

The project `AGENTS.md` names its record, startup order, glossary status and completion condition or owner question. Intake files reflect sourced facts. A completed in-scope packet has a citation-cleared and validated complaint, current official forms and a verified payment or waiver route. For a held request, the labeled handoff names the supported work, each unavailable item and the evidence needed to proceed. The file distinguishes prepared, filed, accepted and served states and records only supported dates and triggered obligations.
