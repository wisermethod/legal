# Maintain Matter File

## Context

Use at the start of any matter-specific session and for “what is due,” “what is overdue,” "I filed," "we received an order," "the opposing party filed," "audit the case files," "clean up the matter file," "tidy the file," "archive old drafts," "what are our arguments," or "what do we need in discovery." Apply the legal constitution's Standing constraint. This skill maintains the matter's records. Case Audit owns evaluation of the whole litigation position.

Classifier seam: none.

## Objective

Leave a checkable record of what happened, where the primary documents sit, what deadlines changed, and which questions still need review. For deadline review, preflight and inventory-only work, return findings without writing record edits.

## Inputs

- `<user_request>` names the matter and desired operation, such as Deadline review, preflight, drafting or research references, record update, inventory audit, argument consolidation, or discovery tracking.
- `<context>` names the owning root, the matter's `AGENTS.md`, current date and any glossary the owning root designates.
- `<source_material>` holds the reported action, dated document, receipt or communication, applicable orders or rules, and any research or evidence newly identified.

## Steps

Give the [Handoff label](../../AGENTS.md#handoff-label) in the handoff message whenever this work returns an assessment, packet or draft to the person, including on an early return. Never write it inside a document.

### What can proceed on this entry?

For **Deadline review**, apply the Standing constraint and run the read below directly. It supplies the deadline findings to [Litigation Counsel's preflight](../../experts/Litigation%20Counsel/EXPERT.md#what-matter-and-deadline-govern). Reuse current findings when the caller already ran this read. When this read gives the alert or answer itself, first apply that preflight's working-period filter and its missing-declaration line. For other operations, follow [Litigation Counsel's preflight contract](../../experts/Litigation%20Counsel/EXPERT.md#how-does-a-skill-request-and-apply-preflight) and use its deadline findings.

### Deadline review (read-only)

Use for “what is due,” “what is overdue” and the first alert on every matter request, including a resume. Identify the owning matter and its standing do-nots. If the matter cannot be identified, surface any urgent date supplied, then ask which matter to read. For a new or undeclared matter use supplied intake or originals and name the missing record. Read the designated deadline record and the available dated orders, docket or administration entries, receipts, notices and service proof needed to check it. Check dated primary records for obligations a stale or missing schedule may omit.

When a status summary and an applicable dated primary record disagree, use the dated record and identify the stale file, line or entry in the answer. A status section that calls itself stale is not proof of posture, even where no dated record contradicts it. Name the section and do not report its account of posture as current. Name the dated order, docket entry or record that would show the current posture, or say that the file holds none. A date in that section that would belong in group 1 or 2 is still shown there, marked `verify`. If two rows concern the same clock, link the readings in the answer and give each its own date or trigger and source. When operative evidence settles which reading governs, identify it and show the displaced reading as `superseded` in the answer only. Otherwise show both as `verify`, name the authority or trigger evidence needed and hold the dependent timeliness conclusion. Check rule version, applicability and computation before confirming a calculated date. Read earlier deadline records as they stand. Recover missing fields from their evidence where possible and identify the fields that still require verification.

When you name a status section that calls itself stale, add one line after the startup alert offering **Clean up the matter file** as a separate operation.

Give the first two groups as the startup alert before the requested work, or state what the available evidence permits you to say about their absence. For a standalone deadline question, give all six groups in the same turn. Put each row in exactly one group. First decide the date type from the evidence, not from the row's label: a conditional date whose trigger has occurred is a fixed or calculated obligation from that trigger, and is grouped as one. Group 4 takes an unapproved requested date, a personal reminder, and a conditional date whose trigger is still unmet. Group 5 takes resolved rows. Every other row is a fixed or calculated obligation and goes in the first of groups 1, 2, 3 or 6 that fits. A fixed or calculated date that may be overdue or falls inside the next 14 days goes in group 1 or 2 even when its reading is still `verify`: show it there with the `verify` state and what would confirm it, and never leave it out of the startup alert. Beside a group 1 or 2 row, state in one line any offer, proposal or request, sent or received and still open, that the deadline record, the correspondence index or a record already opened for this review ties to that date, and any allowance an order or rule already opened for this review gives that the record shows unused and that ends on it, each with its source and as open, never as a change to the date. A draft that was never sent is not an offer, and this line opens no further records. After the two alert groups, or after the statement of their absence, name in one line each group 4 row dated from today through 14 days ahead, as a reminder, a requested date or a conditional step and never as an obligation, with its purpose, pending approval or unmet trigger.

1. Overdue fixed or calculated obligations, and those the available evidence indicates may have been missed. Flag uncertainty and name the risk and prompt licensed-counsel trigger for any urgent potential or verified fatal deadline.
2. Open fixed or calculated obligations with an operative or `verify` date from today through 14 days ahead.
3. Open fixed or calculated obligations with a later operative date.
4. Unapproved requested dates, conditional dates whose trigger is still unmet, and personal reminders, including near or past dates. Identify the pending approval, unmet trigger or reminder purpose. Keep any documented completion or expiry visible in the row’s outcome.
5. Other completed, expired or superseded rows. Distinguish fulfilled obligations, documented expirations and displaced readings, with dates and evidence for each.
6. Other rows that could not be verified locally and any limits on the review. Name unchecked sources, the latest records checked and what could change the answer. Say `local-only` when the live docket or forum record is unconfirmed.

Sort dated items within each group by date. Every row has an obligation or event, its date or pending trigger, owner (the litigant, the other side, the forum or a third party), date type (fixed by order or rule, calculated, requested, conditional or personal reminder), trigger, source locator and state. Identify the specific owner when known. A past date alone establishes neither completion nor expiration.

Return the findings and end the deadline review here. Ask for missing evidence if useful. Perform no writes, request no write approval and skip the update procedure below, even when the register is missing or stale. Follow [Owner review](../../experts/AGENTS.md#owner-review) for a read-only answer.

### Which matter record needs attention?

1. **Locate authoritative destinations.** Use the project’s designated history, deadlines, indexes, glossary and writing conventions. The template paths apply where the matter adopts them. If a path conflicts or is undeclared, locate the authoritative record or report the unresolved choice before writing. Resolve posture from the latest applicable dated event and its underlying evidence. Use a status summary when its evidence establishes the operative event, never on the word of a section that calls itself stale. If the deadline record is missing, return the sourced proposed rows and ask for the authoritative path or confirmation to restore it in update work. Continue independently supported updates and name the pending write in their read-back. Use Litigation Counsel’s forum finding for procedural checks.
2. **Choose the operation.**
   - **Preflight for drafting, procedural work or research.** Check the matter's case law, statutes, claims, arguments and discovery list. Read the designated glossary for defined terms, where present. If the owning root designates none, report that there is no bound glossary and continue the records preflight. Use the practice-area guidance from the preflight only where the requested work needs substantive judgment. Identify the applicable phase from the posture without starting a phase run on a preflight request, such as Pleadings, Discovery, Motions Practice, Settlement Negotiation, Trial Preparation, Venue Selection or Case Audit. Does one of those phase skills address this proceeding? Yes: name it. No: return `phase: unsupported` with the proceeding named, complete the records preflight and carry a hold only to the work that depends on a phase skill. Confirm the procedural posture from the history for response questions. Return the checked sources, dates and gaps to the caller.
   - **Deadline-only update.** Use for a newly learned obligation or a correction that needs no new event. Take the obligation, existing row or identifier if present, due date or trigger, primary source locator, calculation or unresolved inputs, and explicit authorization for the proposed write. Every new or changed row carries the **Deadline review** fields and exactly one state. Use `open` for a supported outstanding obligation, `satisfied` for confirmed completion or a documented expiration with no remaining duty, `superseded` with the source that displaced the prior reading, and `verify` for an unresolved source, trigger, owner or calculation. Label the outcome as completed or expired and cite its evidence. A passed due date alone leaves a possible missed obligation. Keep a completed event and any resulting notice or obligation in separate linked rows. Match by obligation, owner and trigger. Reuse an identical row. For a supported change or new obligation, apply only the approved row changes in the declared deadline record. Explicitly mark any displaced text superseded and link it to the operative row. Where competing readings remain, retain both with their sources and request the evidence that resolves the conflict before changing the record. For an ambiguous match, an unavailable destination or absent approval, return the exact proposed rows with unresolved claims marked `[verify]`. Preserve the order, index and history of the underlying event. Read back the changed rows and their sources and report `applied`, `unchanged` or `pending`.
   - **Our filing.** Confirm the document name and actual filing date against the receipt, docket or supplied confirmation. The person’s own statement that a paper was filed is a report, never a confirmation. If filing is unconfirmed, keep the report and missing confirmation in pending history, under the history record’s open questions where it has no pending section, hold the actual filings index and dependent updates, and ask for the stamped copy or receipt. For a confirmed filing with the copy available, preserve the copy in the declared filings directory under its filename rule, or reuse the exact filed copy already present. Use `YYYY-MM-DD - <Document Name>.<ext>` only where the template pattern applies. When a receipt or other confirmation identifies the filing but the copy is missing, record the confirmed date and receipt or docket locator in the designated filings index and history. Mark the file field as missing and request the exact filed copy. When the copy arrives, match it to the same receipt or docket identifier, confirm any uncertain match, and complete the existing entries. Use Event, Outcome and Documents in history. Update deadlines the evidence shows the filing created or satisfied and Current Status when posture changes.
   - **Court or forum order or ruling.** Which evidence arrived? If the order is available, confirm its issue date and preserve it under the declaration’s filename rule in its designated orders directory. Where the template pattern applies, use `YYYY-MM-DD - Order <Description>.<ext>`. Update the designated orders index, history with the ruling and implications, and Current Status. If only a notice identifies the event and date, record both in history and the orders index with the notice locator and missing-order note, and ask for the order. If the notice cannot identify the event, record the notice in pending history and ask which order it concerns. In every branch, apply a deadline only where the supplied primary evidence and governing rule support it. Ask whether the litigant wants to discuss next steps or implications. Explain evidence-based options and tradeoffs within the confirmed procedural posture when asked.
   - **Opposing party's filing.** Confirm the document and date. Preserve it under the declaration’s filename rule in its designated filings directory. Where the template pattern applies, use `YYYY-MM-DD - <Party> <Document Name>.<ext>`. Update the designated filings index and history. Determine whether a response is required and note any applicable verified deadline in the deadline record. Ask whether the litigant plans to respond, and show the verified date or say what remains unresolved. If response duty or date remains unclear, ask which response is contemplated and what authority or notice sets its deadline.
   - **Service confirmed.** Use dated proof or other authoritative confirmation to record the served document, actual date, method, recipient and location where it affects a deadline. Link the proof in history and the applicable index. If the proof was filed, use **Our filing** for that separate event and link it to the service record. Apply supported response deadlines, keep uncertain inputs pending and read back both event links and the deadline. A proposed or unconfirmed service action stays pending.
   - **Correspondence sent or received.** Capture date, direction, parties, subject, source and path in the correspondence index and history. Record any resulting deadline and update Current Status when affected. A proposal to add, move or satisfy a date that is not yet effective, such as the other side’s offer of more time, does not change a deadline: record it as open correspondence, keep the existing date in force, and change the date only when the record holds what makes the change effective in that forum. A forum’s instruction, an extension the forum itself granted, or a request that itself sets a response date under a rule already in the record is a resulting deadline and is recorded as one. The other side’s agreement to more time stays an open proposal until the record shows it is effective in that forum. Drafted or scheduled correspondence stays distinct from a completed transmission.
   - **"Audit the case files" or "check for gaps."** Enumerate filings, orders, evidence and correspondence. Compare both ways with history and the applicable indexes. Report missing files, unindexed files, unsupported events, date mismatches and duplicate or broken links. Propose concrete fixes and apply them only on approval. For "how is the case going," "audit the case" or "evaluate our position," run Case Audit with the entire matter file set and evidence. Use its full method and bring the findings and prioritized actions back without substituting an inventory summary.
   - **Clean up the matter file.** Use when the person requests matter file cleanup. Follow [cleanup.md](cleanup.md) through its handoff and return from this operation.
   - **"What are our arguments?" or "consolidate arguments."** Read claims and current arguments. Read both sides' filings for positions, attacks and rebuttals. Consolidate the narrative theme, each side's contentions, the cited evidence, weaknesses and open work in the arguments record. Link to the claims and original record rather than creating rival versions of facts. Retain abandoned or weakened arguments with their status.
   - **Discovery queue.** Use for "what do we need in discovery?" or "add to discovery list." Load the matter's discovery requests. Accept Discovery's verified category, reason, source, identification date, priority and confirmation. Preserve those values. For a directly identified need, classify it as a document request, interrogatory, request for admission, third-party subpoena or deposition and collect the same fields. Show additions and ask for approval where it is missing. Reuse an identical entry and read back each approved change. Return the exact pending entries when approval or the declared destination is missing. When any matter work reveals information needed from the other side, ask whether to add it.
   - **Different or unclear operation.** Ask which listed operation the person wants, keep deadline alerts visible and return before any write.
3. **Write and reconcile.** Respect the declaration’s file-shape rules. When one file owns dated facts, update that file once and link to it from other records. Apply generic history and filename patterns only where the owning matter uses them. What supports the update? If original records and reported facts agree, use the primary source and record the dates, source and status. If only the person's report is available, label the basis and any missing file or receipt. If records conflict, preserve both readings, flag the conflict and ask before changing a contested fact. Check existing paths and index entries before copying or appending. Reuse identical existing files and update one entry per event. If a path holds different bytes, preserve both and obtain a distinct path before adding the new copy. Keep orders’ issuance, entry and service dates separate when relevant. Apply the Deadline-only update row shape and states to event-related deadline entries. Check rule version, trigger, applicable forum modification and calculation before treating a deadline as confirmed. Read back the changed paths, history links, indexes, deadline status and Current Status. Never silently skip a record update after an event. If a required update cannot be made, name the specific file and entry owed, its evidence, the blocker and what clears it in the handoff.
4. **Maintain reusable knowledge on confirmation.** Show the confirmed finding, its source and the exact proposed addition. Ask before writing it. Store case authority and statutes or rules in their designated matter records, and reusable definitions in the owning root’s designated writable glossary. If a glossary has no designated home, ask for one. Link evidence through the matter’s own evidence record. Use plain English, a precise definition where needed, typical context and the encounter date or source for glossary entries. When a reference is thin, research the gap and propose the supported addition in the matching writable record. Improvements to an expert’s reference material belong to the plugin maintainer.

### What should a phase do with a deadline?

Lead phase analysis with the alert from the current entry preflight. Run a fresh Deadline review when the facts change and re-alert when new evidence reveals urgency. Mark uncertain dates `[verify]` and hold readiness or timeliness conclusions that depend on them. For each learned or changed date, send the obligation, existing row if any, deadline, owner, date type, trigger, primary locator, calculation or open inputs, proposed state and write authorization. Use the applicable event operation for a confirmed new filing, order or communication. Use **Deadline-only update** when the event is already recorded or the date has no new event. Include the read-back or exact pending entry in the phase packet. If the event write is unavailable or unauthorized, include the exact proposed entry with its source and status.

## Pitfalls

- If the matter, operation or destination is ambiguous, read the owning instruction chain first and ask before changing records. Blanket acceptance of recommendations does not confirm an undisclosed correction, discovery item or glossary addition.
- A source file is evidence. Keep its bytes intact and work from copies. Index the registered path and use links to the record's single source of truth.

## Success

The person sees the first two deadline groups, or the statement of their absence, before other work, followed by one line for each group 4 row dated from today through 14 days ahead, named as a reminder, a requested date or a conditional step and never as an obligation. A standalone Deadline review returns all six groups in the same turn, with each sourced, typed row in one group, stale lines and competing readings identified, and local limits visible. It leaves the matter unchanged and asks for no write approval. A completed update identifies the original, the actual date and the applicable index, history and deadline entries. Read-back resolves those references or names the affected write and what will clear it. A preflight, ambiguous request or other held update returns a labeled handoff with its findings, pending edits, missing items, conflicts and any discovery or glossary additions awaiting approval.
