# Produce Court Filing

## Context

Use to produce or render a court-ready motion, complaint or other filing in the litigant's own matter. Apply the legal constitution's Standing constraint. Use the jurisdiction profile and the court filing pipeline for a compatible court PDF. Use the forum’s stated format for a submission with no compatible profile.

Classifier seam: none.

## Objective

Produce the exact artifact in the forum's required format and keep the build record that supports preview, approval and filing status.

## Inputs

- `<user_request>` identifies the matter, document, format and planned date of filing or service.
- `<context>` names the matter `AGENTS.md`, court, jurisdiction profile, build and output paths, applicable page or word limit, and any planned submission or service route.
- `<source_material>` includes approved substantive `body.md`, structural `caption.json` for a profiled build or a sourced forum submission record with the fields described in Draft Motion’s unprofiled path, the current citation report, final attachments with the Verify Citations cited-attachment manifest (possibly empty), and authoritative caption and service sources. When something is missing, obtain it through the relevant drafting workflow before the dependent step.

## Steps

Give the [Handoff label](../../AGENTS.md#handoff-label) in the handoff message whenever this work returns an assessment, packet or draft to the person, including on an early return. Never write it inside a document.

### What can proceed on this entry?

Follow [Litigation Counsel's preflight contract](../../experts/Litigation%20Counsel/EXPERT.md#how-does-a-skill-request-and-apply-preflight) before dependent work.

### Which production path applies?

1. **Stage the build and choose the output.** If preflight holds production, return the held draft and blocker. Otherwise run Maintain Matter File in preflight mode. Read the forum’s operative orders, notices, submission instructions and prescribed formats. Select a compatible profiled PDF, a prescribed official original, or a confirmed unprofiled format. A Word submission can use a compatible profile for its proof under step 4. If the requirements or format conflict or are missing, hold the affected work. Resolve tools through the composed family indexes and follow their install contracts. Run Court Filing `profiles` for a court artifact and read its `profiles` array. Use `colorado-district` only on the confirmed Colorado district court route. Select the `document_types` entry, packet and applicable length choice from that profile and the controlling record. Use the profile’s `validation.limits` for working preset figures. Record the authority for the chosen limit, an explicit override or a verified unlimited type.

   Keep the paper's real title in `caption.json`. Choose the specific type when the title fits and the controlling requirements agree. For a Colorado district single paper without a specific match, choose `other-paper` with its filer signature and certificate of service. Use both general motion limits and tell the litigant which page and word ceilings were applied. If the tool flags separate papers joined in one title, check the intended documents and build each paper separately. Never retitle a paper to satisfy a pattern.

   Use the declared visible filings directory and `YYYY-MM-DD - <Document Title>.<required extension>` with the planned filing or service date. Reserve `DRAFT - ` for editable working sources. Keep sources and evidence in a dedicated portable build directory under filings. Use one build record for every path. Record input digests and exact paths, each applicable requirement and its authority, commands or inspections and results, output digests, preview, the human decision and the release state. For a collision preserve the existing file and use the first unused permitted revision of the date/title stem, starting with ` - 2` before the extension. If the exact name is mandatory, keep the staged review artifact in the build and confirm a fresh authorized destination before publishing it.

2. **Clear substance, caption and citation.** Keep argument, facts, citations, conferral when required and prayer in `body.md`.

   On the profiled route use `caption.json` for fields such as court and address, party roles and names, exact case number, division, document title, signer name, role and contact lines, and service method, signer, recipients and firm details when applicable. Take field names, requirements and defaults from the selected profile. Reconcile parties and dates with the docket and orders, and the firm and service information with counsel's latest filing or communication. Hold for any required discrepancy or missing field. Use exact assigned docket values. For a Colorado district initial complaint use `document_type: "new-case-complaint"`, `filing_packet: "new_case_complaint"` and `case_number: ""`. Confirm whether the court accepts `division: ""` for a pending assignment. Supply `service` when required by the type, including for the initial complaint. The Colorado `supporting-declaration` type requires `filing_packet: "motion_response_with_declaration"` and verification that the enclosing response’s certificate covers it. Use `proposed-order` only when the controlling form matches the tool’s unsigned judicial closing. A custom type that omits a certificate on the basis of an exact title requires the controlling source and matching facts. Hold for unresolved treatment, impermissible blanks or service facts that cannot be truthfully attested.

   For other paths use the authoritative forum submission fields and record any unresolved requirement. Leave signature and required service dates to the signer.

   For every production path, check citation clearance. Match the exact `cleared` value, `full` scope, current body path and SHA-256, and each cited attachment's identifier, original path, SHA-256 and complete coverage to the manifest and actual submission inputs. Compare the manifest to the draft references and final attachments. If the current report is `held`, carry its holds forward and rerun only when corrected text, an updated attachment, or new authority addresses a finding. For a missing, incomplete or stale report, run Verify Citations on the exact body and manifest with originals and any readable companion text. Hold production until there is a matching clearance and no unresolved `[verify]`. Confirm the litigant has approved the substantive version for rendering.
3. **Build a profiled PDF or PDF proof.** On the profiled PDF path or when step 4 requires a compatible proof, use Court Filing and Wiser’s `render` in this order. Otherwise continue to step 4.
   1. From Court Filing run `node scripts/court-filing.js compose --body <body.md> --caption <caption.json> --profile <id-or-file> --out <fresh.html>` with absolute file arguments. Keep the resulting `html`, `profile`, `pdf`, `pdfArgs`, `footerFont`, `bodySha256` and `bodyWords`. Match `bodySha256` to the citation clearance before rendering.
   2. From the `wiser` tool `render` run `node scripts/render.js pdf --input <html> --output <fresh.pdf>` and append `pdfArgs` unchanged as individual arguments. Require a successful run with `output` at the intended path, `format` equal to `pdf`, and a concrete `pages` count. Keep the CSS page geometry and footer settings from the composition. If the renderer refuses the file, hold and resolve the named error before retrying.
   3. From Court Filing run `node scripts/court-filing.js validate --pdf <output> --caption <caption.json> --profile <id-or-file> --body-sha256 <compose digest> --body <body.md>` with the same inputs. For `other-paper`, omit length flags to apply both general ceilings. Use explicit maxima only to tighten those ceilings and record the applied values from the result. For other limited types, pass `--limit <id>` for a listed preset or `--max-pages <N>` and/or `--max-words <N>` for verified explicit ceilings. Pass the standalone flag `--no-length-limit` only when the selected type has null `length_presets` and the applicable rule or order confirms it. Require exit 0, `ok: true`, and exactly `result: "ALL CHECKS PASSED"`. Match `profile`, `documentType`, `bodySha256`, and `limits.choice`, `limits.preset`, `limits.pages` and `limits.words` to the selected inputs. Record `pdfSha256`, `pages`, `bodyWords`, `pdfBodyWords`, `checks` and `unavailable`. Require the independently re-parsed body comparison and second metadata check to pass, along with the selected length checks, printed caption and layout, and all blocks required by the type. Accept certificate `not_applicable` only with the passing absence check and sourced treatment. For a required blank case number, require the named blank-field check to pass. On exit 1, read stderr for the failure and retain it in the build record. Record any `remediation` field and hold on any failed or required unchecked item, absent length choice or mismatched input. Carry advisory checks into preview. Changes to the text or cited attachments reopen citation clearance.
4. **Build and check a Word, official-form or other unprofiled artifact when required.** A simple new DOCX uses Wiser’s `word-document` command `node scripts/word-document.js create --input <json> --output <fresh.docx>` with absolute paths and UTF-8 JSON containing `title` and `paragraphs`. Require success and the intended canonical `output`. For a required caption table or other structure outside that schema, use the issuing forum’s official Word template or a Word artifact that meets the prescribed structure. Retain a legacy table caption as a visual reference when supplied. For an official fillable form, obtain and fill the original and check its required fields and packet rules. Keep a prescribed order with a different judicial closing in its original format. For another confirmed submission format, use a local tool that supports the verified requirements. For a required Word filing with an applicable compatible court profile, use step 3 to retain a mechanically validated PDF proof and reconcile caption, text, page structure and certification against the actual DOCX. For an unprofiled forum or prescribed original outside the profile’s scope, record Court Filing validation as unavailable for that artifact. Profile-only evidence is inapplicable there. If a required compatible proof or a check is unavailable, name the hold.

   On every path, check the final artifact against the cleared body and primary record. Record each applicable requirement’s authority, evidence and pass or hold, covering text, layout, limits, fields, attachments, audience, signature and service. Use the original official forms and packet rules for the Colorado initiation packet. If Open a Matter handed over only the complaint, return the complaint for its JDF 601, JDF 600 and fee or waiver checks. Route a direct request for that entire packet to Open a Matter.
5. **Review the exact artifact with the litigant.** For PDF, rasterize every page to PNG in the build folder with a local tool such as `pdftoppm -png` and inspect the pages against the build record. For DOCX, reopen the exact output in Word or another layout-capable Word viewer without editing or resaving it. Check the visible page count and inspect every page against the sourced requirements. For another prescribed format, use its layout-capable viewer. Check applicable caption, spacing, page numbers, margins, exhibits, signature and certificate treatment. For a profiled PDF, tell the litigant the count and share in the validation check `Table, list and heading double spacing` and show any flagged elements in the preview. Hold with the missing viewer or check named if a required preview cannot be completed. Show the artifact, intended submission, recipients and attachments. Record the SHA-256 of each submitted file. The profiled PDF hash must match the passing `pdfSha256`.

   The litigant signs and completes the applicable blank dates by the forum’s accepted method. Verify the planned filename date against the actual filing or service date. After a signature or date edit, repeat the applicable gate and affected preview on the new bytes, then obtain approval of the new hashes. For a profiled PDF use the same body, digest, caption, profile and length choice and record the new `pdfSha256`. If the editor strips the body binding, use an accepted method that preserves it or hold. Changes to body or cited attachments reopen citation clearance and the applicable build and review steps.
6. **Record release and filing separately.** Apply the Litigation Counsel owner gate under `wiser/AGENTS.md` Precedence and routing before any filing-ready handoff, subject to its separate-review decline path. Carry any preflight hold even when that review is declined. Is the exact reviewed packet explicitly approved for transmission with all applicable gates passed? If yes, the litigant can file through the forum’s approved channel, including Colorado Courts E-Filing where applicable, or give a specific instruction to transmit the exact shown packet where permitted. If approval is absent, return the validated artifact for the litigant's review. Record the person’s approval with the reviewed hashes, destination and attachments. Check those hashes again at handoff or any authorized transmission. Reopen the affected gate, preview and approval for any changed artifact. After the litigant reports filing or supplies the court receipt, use Maintain Matter File to record the actual date and update the matter. Retain previews and build sources as scratch under the matter's retention rules.

### What does a phase send for filing production?

For a proposed filing or forum submission, send the exact filing text and cited-attachment manifest to Verify Citations with the forum, relevant date, supplied authority and any known lookup stop. Use an empty manifest when there are no cited attachments. Carry the occurrence findings, pinpoints and any lookup stop and next step in the phase packet. Check available independent primary material and close each unresolved `[verify]` before a filing-ready handoff. Recheck clearance on changed text or cited attachments.

Check standing orders for AI disclosure and citation certification. Reconcile the parties, exact docket fields, dates, counsel and firm names and service addresses against the docket, orders and latest counsel filing or communication. Use the initial-filing rules above where applicable. For a requested artifact, pass the approved `body.md`, matching clearance and manifest, final attachments, sourced caption and service evidence, declared build and output paths, requested format, planned date and verified limit where applicable. Supply the profile and structural `caption.json` for a profiled artifact or the sourced forum submission record for another format. Hold for any required missing date, attachment or unresolved fact and retain the blocked draft. Use the applicable production and visual checks above before a readiness claim.

## Pitfalls

- A vague document, conflicting jurisdiction, or unclear date needs clarification before rendering. A date slip calls for updating the visible filename and reviewing any date-dependent fields.
- Review the actual printed pages and compare the signature, service fields, spacing and geometry against the governing requirements. Report tool or profile defects to their owning tool. Never patch the tool during filing production.
- Colorado's e-filing channel, a completed signature block and a preview can each exist before filing. Record filed status only when supported by confirmation.

## Success

There is a visible artifact at a fresh path or a named hold with its evidence. The build record binds citation clearance, applicable requirements and the preview and decision to the exact output bytes. No filing can be released with a held gate or recorded as filed without confirmation. Ask the litigant to report back after filing so the matter file can be updated.
