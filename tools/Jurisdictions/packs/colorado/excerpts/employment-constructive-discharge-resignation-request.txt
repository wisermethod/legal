A request for a resignation will not support a claim of constructive discharge unless accompanied by harassment, coercion, or other employer conduct which makes the working conditions intolerable. Knee v. School District No. 139, 106 Idaho 152, 676 P.2d 727 (App.1984). Even though the employer may have a motive to force an employee's resignation, the employee's knowledge that his job is protected by statute and contract militates against finding a constructive discharge. Knee v. School District No. 139, supra.
