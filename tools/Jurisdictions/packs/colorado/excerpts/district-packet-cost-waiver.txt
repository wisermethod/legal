13-16-103. Costs of poor person. (1) If the judge or justice of any court, including the
supreme court, is at any time satisfied that any person is unable to prosecute or defend any civil
action or special proceeding because he is a poor person and unable to pay the costs and
expenses thereof, the judge or justice, in his discretion, may permit such person to commence
and prosecute or defend an action or proceeding without the payment of costs; but, in the event
such person prosecutes or defends an action or proceeding successfully, there shall be a
judgment entered in his favor for the amount of court costs which he would have incurred except
for the provision of this section, and this judgment shall be first satisfied out of any money paid
into court, and such costs shall be paid to the court before any such judgment is satisfied of
record.
