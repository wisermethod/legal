13-6-404. Clerk of the small claims court. The clerk of the county court or a deputy
designated by said clerk shall act as the clerk of the small claims court. The clerk of the small
claims court shall provide such assistance as may be requested by any person regarding the
jurisdiction, operations, and procedures of the small claims court; however, the clerk shall not
engage in the practice of law. All necessary forms shall be available from the clerk.
