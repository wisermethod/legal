To prevail on a claim for breach of contract, a party must show a contract was in existence and that the other party failed to perform some term of the contract. Spencer Invs., Inc. v. Bohn, 923 P.2d 140 (Colo.App. 1995). The interpretation of a contract is a question of law that we review de novo. Rocky Mountain Health Maint. Org., Inc. v. Colo. Dep't of Health Care Policy & Fin., 54 P.3d 913 (Colo.App.2001).
