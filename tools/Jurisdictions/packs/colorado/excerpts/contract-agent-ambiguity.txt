The law is that any ambiguity in an instrument whereby it is sought to hold a purported agent personally liable, can be explained by parol evidence. In such a situation the court will take the entire contract into consideration, not the signature alone. See Fricke v. Belz, 237 Mo.App. 861, 177 S.W.2d 702 (1944); Butterick Co. v. Molen, 192 Okl. 602, 138 P.2d 89 (1943); Pennsylvania Co. for Insurance of Lives and Granting of Annuities v. Wallace, 346 Pa. 532, 31 A.2d 71, 156 A.L.R. 1 (1943); Consumers' Twine & Machinery Co. v. Mt. Pleasant Thermo Tank Co., 196 Iowa 64, 194 N.W. 290 [161 Colo. 348] (1923); Hurricane Milling Co. v. Steel & Payne Co., 84 W.Va. 376, 99 S.E. 490, 6 A.L.R. 637 (1919).
