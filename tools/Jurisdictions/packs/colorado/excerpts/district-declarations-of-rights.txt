13-51-105. Power and force of declaration. Courts of record within their respective
jurisdictions have power to declare rights, status, and other legal relations whether or not further
relief is or could be claimed. No action or proceeding shall be open to objection on the ground
that a declaratory judgment or decree is prayed for. The declaration may be either affirmative or
