# Federal district court forum

This pack supplies national civil procedure for the United States district courts. Jurisdictions recognizes the full court name and supported conventional district abbreviations. The matter's governing law by claim remains a separate declaration. The pack suggests no filing profile.

## Sources and dates

- The Federal Rules of Civil Procedure are from the United States Courts book as amended to December 1, 2025. The national rules page listed that text as current when the official sources were collected on 2026-11-23.
- The statutory excerpts are from the Office of the Law Revision Counsel's current U.S. Code pages, laws in effect through Public Law 119-142 (November 10, 2026), read on 2026-11-23. Check any later applicable amendments or effective-date changes before use.
- The excerpt from *Caterpillar Inc. v. Lewis*, 519 U.S. 61, 68 (1996), supplies the complete-diversity rule. Use the § 1332 excerpt for the amount in controversy.

Each statement identifies its source, read date and saved excerpt. Use the cited text and applicable exceptions when checking a procedure.

## Sources to obtain for the matter

Save each governing source with its citation or locator, date read and effective date or version if given. Use the matter's existing **Rules home** for the district's current local civil rules and amendments, judge's individual practices, applicable standing and case-specific orders, clerk filing and e-service instructions, forms and fee schedules. Read those matter records first and use any applicable local rule or order that lawfully changes a national default. Confirm the matter's **Filing profile** separately.

Court-specific gaps include opposition and reply briefing periods, page and word limits, font and other layout requirements, filing and e-service eligibility and registration, forms, current filing or waiver fees and the assigned judge's requirements. Rule 6(c)'s periods govern hearing papers and affidavits as specified in that rule. Obtain the applicable briefing schedule separately. Obtain the actual hearing location and calendar from the docket, notice or court.

Claim-specific jurisdictional grants and exclusivity, ordinary diversity aggregation, transfer choice-of-law consequences, and decisions interpreting the removal clock need their own applicable primary authorities. For any appointment or tenure comparison, obtain the source for the particular judicial office. The pack supplies the rules and excerpts identified in its forum file.
