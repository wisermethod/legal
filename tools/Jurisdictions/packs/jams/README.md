# JAMS employment arbitration

This pack summarizes Rules 1 through 34 of the JAMS Employment Arbitration Rules and Procedures effective 2021-06-01, read 2026-11-23. Each statement links to its full saved rule for authoritative detail. The pack covers forum procedure. Governing law is resolved from the matter's own declaration and applicable authorities.

A declaration of `JAMS` identifies this pack as a source to check. Rule 1 governs when the employment rules apply. Under Rule 3 the applicable edition depends on the Rule 5 Commencement Letter date or the parties' agreement. The matter's agreement, any agreed procedures and case directions determine which requirements apply.

The 34 saved rules contain no court-style caption layout or numerical page or word limit. They do specify content and form for particular documents, including service copies, notices, pre-hearing submissions and awards. Case scheduling orders and arbitrator directions may specify submission format and length.
