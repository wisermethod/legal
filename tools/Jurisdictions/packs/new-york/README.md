## Governing-law limits

- **Contract.** Obtain authority for general formation, interpretation, breach accrual and additional damages doctrines. Obtain the applicable text for CPLR 213-a and 214-i, UCC Article 2 and General Business Law Article 36-B before relying on the cited exceptions. Choices outside the covered commercial choice-of-law provision need their own authority. The captured General Obligations Law 5-1401 text uses “household services”; the pack preserves that wording.
- **Consumer.** Obtain General Business Law 350-a and current controlling opinions on private deception and false advertising, including the effect of the 2026 changes to section 349 and the continuing reach of earlier precedent. Claim-specific limitations, accrual, territorial reach, injury and materiality and specialized consumer regimes need their own authority.
- **Employment.** Obtain Labor Law 193 regulations, Banking Law 14-a and the applicable rate materials, Labor Law 198-c wage supplement authority, and current authority on contested private pay-frequency remedies. Minimum wage, overtime, leave and industry-specific rules need separate sources. The cross-reference from Labor Law 198(1-d)(ii) to 195(1)(e) in the wage-statement defense remains unresolved. Obtain the omitted notice, posting, remedy, venue and jury provisions in Labor Law 195(5), 198(1) and (4), 215(1)(b) and (3), 740 and 201-d as applicable. The separate automatic 15 percent judgment-increase remedy in Labor Law 198(4) is not summarized here; consult its complete conditions in the [saved section](excerpts/law-employment-article-6-limitations.txt).
- **State Human Rights Law.** The employment files cover selected discrimination, harassment, accommodation, retaliation and relief provisions. Obtain additional authority for accommodation issues beyond those statements, nonemployee and intern coverage, specialized hiring, and claim-specific agency procedure, judicial accrual and limitations. Housing discrimination is outside this coverage.
- **Historical application.** Obtain the amendment acts and applicable decisions for the 2026 General Business Law 349 and Executive Law 296 changes, the Labor Law 198(1-a)(ii) trigger, the Executive Law 297(5) filing-period transition, and the CPLR 5004 rate amendment and separate subdivision (c) date.

## Civil trial forum limits

Use an explicit New York state Supreme Court and county name in a supported form, such as `Supreme Court of the State of New York, County of Kings`. The [manifest](pack.json) lists the recognized forms. A match locates a civil trial procedure file. A miss leaves the procedure lookup unresolved. Subject-matter jurisdiction, personal jurisdiction and venue still require their own sources and facts. Other New York courts are outside this forum coverage. Federal district courts, including the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, are separate forums.

The forum file covers selected initiation, process and service, pleading, motion, default, venue, paper format and e-filing topics. Use its [obtain list](forum/supreme-court.json) to identify the applicable county, assigned part and judge rules, case orders, NYSCEF authorization and instructions, signatures, redaction, original forms, fees and waiver materials. It also identifies the additional jurisdiction authorities, CPLR article 3 methods, venue exceptions, special proceedings, and pleading, discovery and default requirements to obtain. A saved source can contain provisions beyond the supported statements. Use the operative original and verify the particular provision before applying it.

Commercial Division matters need their applicable rules and assigned justice’s instructions, including the separate Rule 17 treatment for length and certification. They are outside the ordinary New York State Supreme Court filing profile. The profile covers only eligible counsel-filed motion papers in assigned ordinary civil cases submitted through NYSCEF. For an uncovered paper, use the court’s original form or other verified requirements and hold only the dependent steps.
