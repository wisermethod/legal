# Coverage gaps

The typed files own these coverage limits and the conditions that hold work. Court rules and source reading dates in the linked references govern when a lead can be used.

## Experts

### [Litigation Counsel](../experts/Litigation%20Counsel/EXPERT.md)

- No arbitration rules reference is shipped. Use the governing materials in the matter record.
- Federal courts and courts outside Colorado have no jurisdiction profile.

### [Cease and Desist](../experts/Cease%20and%20Desist/EXPERT.md)

- Cease and Desist is a substantive stub for whether to send or bypass a demand, required terms, deadlines, and response and escalation rules
- Cease and Desist is a substantive stub for IP, defamation, harassment or stalking, contract, property and nuisance grounds and service requirements

### [Consumer Law](../experts/Consumer%20Law/EXPERT.md)

- Consumer Law is a substantive stub for the Colorado Consumer Protection Act, including scope, deceptive practices, elements, damages, and limitations
- Consumer Law is a substantive stub for federal FDCPA, TILA, FCRA, and TCPA provisions, elements, and damages
- Consumer Law has no verified reference on defense tactics, strategic considerations, or Colorado Attorney General, CFPB, and FTC resources

### [Contract Disputes](../experts/Contract%20Disputes/EXPERT.md)

- Verified accrual analysis for a particular contract claim
- Verified Colorado authority for general reliance damages as an alternative contract measure
- Verified authority for Colorado adopting Hadley v. Baxendale by name

### [Employment Law](../experts/Employment%20Law/EXPERT.md)

- Employment coverage is working and lacks official confirmation of the 2025 wage amendments, the current CDLE cap, and a current filing forms and venue check
- Employment briefing lacks complete opinion reads for the flagged cases and verified Colorado authority for notice and cure, prevention of a bonus condition, remote-worker jurisdiction, and unsigned arbitration
- Employment coverage lacks verified out-of-state Cause and severance analysis and CADA or POWER Act elements for protected-class claims
- Employment guidance for unemployment-insurance hearings, workers' compensation, union labor law, discrimination, FMLA, and disability

### [Personal Injury](../experts/Personal%20Injury/EXPERT.md)

- Personal Injury is a substantive stub for Colorado negligence standards, comparative fault, and compensable damages and caps
- Personal Injury is a substantive stub for motor vehicle, premises, and product liability, insurance and UM/UIM, limitations, and defense tactics

### [Jurisdictions](../tools/Jurisdictions/TOOL.md)

- Colorado district court [verify] topics include the scope of civil monetary jurisdiction, geographic venue and transfer, complaint pleading, cover sheet and summons, current docket fee, waiver forms and submission, electronic filing eligibility and mandates, process service interval and methods, proof of service, answer periods and the effect of a pre-answer motion, amendment as of right, case management and trial timing, additional conferral exceptions and consequences of noncompliance, settlement evidence, discovery response periods, and declaratory relief procedure.
- Colorado county court [verify] topics include local complaint filing and electronic filing, the service interval and proof requirements, current docket fees, discovery scope, and when an appeal is heard de novo.
- Colorado small claims [verify] topics include the official complaint form, filing and service, current docket fees, hearing wait, discovery availability, and scope of appeal.
- The Colorado pack still needs official text for the unresolved parts of the Colorado Rules of Civil Procedure, including Rules 4, 8, 12, 15, 16.1, 26 through 37, 57 and 98, Rule 121 sections 1-15 and 1-26, and the 300 and 500 series, plus Colorado Rule of Evidence 408. The saved excerpts cover only the provisions they print. Current forms, local rules, division practice standards and applicable case orders are separate sources to obtain.
- Colorado contract law [verify] topics include general reliance damages outside the sales code and whether Colorado adopted Hadley v. Baxendale by name.
- Colorado employment law [verify] topics include owner liability and retaliation remedies under the 2025 wage amendments, the wage-claim administrative cap effective in 2026, the implied good-faith covenant in at-will employment, prevention of a compensation condition, individual arbitration and class or collective waivers, and mitigation of fixed severance.
- The applicable 2025 and 2026 changes to Colorado court rules and fee statutes still need confirmation against official text. The enacted 2025 wage changes and the operative 2026 cap and its effective date remain unconfirmed.
- Colorado consumer and personal injury law is covered only by the statements the pack holds. Any other issue in those areas still requires authority.
